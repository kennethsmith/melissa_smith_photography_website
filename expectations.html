What to Expect

Your session from start to finish, so there are no surprises.

01

Booking & Planning

Once you reach out through the contact form, I'll respond within 24 hours. We'll chat about what you're looking for, pick a date, and decide on a location. I'll send over a booking contract and invoice for the retainer. Your date isn't secured until the contract is signed and the retainer is paid.

Shortly before your session, I'll send a prep guide with outfit suggestions, what to bring, and location details.

02

The Session

When we meet, you don't need to know how to pose. I'll guide you through everything—where to stand, how to hold hands, when to laugh. My goal is for you to feel like yourself, so we'll chat, joke, and take breaks if the little ones need them.

Sessions typically last 45-60 minutes, and I always schedule for the golden hour (the hour before sunset) for the most flattering light. If you have young kids, rest assured—I've photographed more toddler meltdowns than you've had, and they always end up being the sweetest candids.

03

Editing & Your Gallery

Within 2-3 weeks (up to 6 weeks for weddings), you'll receive a link to your private online gallery. Every image is hand-edited for color, exposure, and clarity—never filters or presets slapped on.

You'll get a curated selection of the best images from your session. No silly blinks, no mid-bite shots, no mistakes you said "please delete this one." You can download high-resolution files, order prints directly from the gallery, or select additional images.

04

Go Behind the Scenes

Want to stay in the loop between sessions? Follow me on Instagram and Facebook for sneak peeks, mini session announcements, and plenty of blooper reels from past sessions.

Quick Tips Before Your Session

Outfits

Coordinate, don't match. Solid colors and earth tones photograph best. Avoid busy patterns and neon — they can cast color onto skin. Layers and textures (knits, linen, denim) add depth.

Kids & Pets

Bring snacks and something to keep them entertained between shots. If your dog is coming, bring treats and a helper who can hold them between poses. Some pets are huge stars; others need a minute to warm up. Both are fine.

Weather

Portland weather is a coin flip. If it looks like rain, we just adjust. Umbrellas and rain boots make adorable photos, and overcast days actually give gorgeous, even light. Extreme conditions (downpours, wind storms) are rescheduled at no cost.

Props

A few personal items can make photos feel uniquely yours — baby's favorite blanket, kid's soccer ball, grandma's quilt. Keep it to 1-2 items so they don't steal the show from the real subject: you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I choose my own location?

Absolutely. I've got a list of tried-and-true spots, but if you have somewhere meaningful—a favorite park, a family property, your neighborhood—let me know and we'll make it work.

What if it rains on my session day?

I'll check the forecast the day before and we'll decide together. Many sessions actually work great in light rain, but if conditions are extreme, we reschedule at no cost. Your retainer transfers to the new date.

How soon will I get my photos?

Most sessions are delivered within 2-3 weeks. Wedding galleries take up to 6 weeks. You'll get a sneak peek on social media within 48 hours.

Can I order prints through you?

Yes! Your online gallery includes direct print ordering. You also receive a print release, so you're free to print wherever you like.

Do you offer payment plans?

Weddings and elopements can be split into monthly payments up to your session date. Just ask when booking.
