Hi, I'm Melissa

I'm a portrait and lifestyle photographer with over three years of experience and over 7 years of experience working in the commercial photography industry.

I've always loved the way a photograph can capture a moment and bring you right back to how it felt. For the past three years, I've had the chance to photograph families, seniors, sports, and events throughout the Pacific Northwest.

My goal is to create images that feel authentic, timeless, and true to the people in them. I want you to have photos you love today and will still love years from now.
