Viking Company Holiday Party

December 2024 | Portland

The Story

The downtown ballroom, decked out for holiday cheer. Twinkling lights, toasts from the executive team, and a dance floor that stayed full until the very last song.

We started with first looks among the vines, floated through the ceremony under the oak grove, and saved the best for a golden-hour walk between the rows of grapes. It rained for exactly seven minutes — just long enough for the "good luck" story.
