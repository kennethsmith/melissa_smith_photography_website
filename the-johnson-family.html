Featured Session

The Johnson Family

October 2024 | Forest Park, Portland

The Story

A crisp autumn morning in the woods with three rambunctious kids and their golden retriever. We chased leaves, dodged mud puddles, and let the kids run until they forgot the camera was there.

Fall in Forest Park is pure magic — the light filters down through golden maples, and the trails are quiet. The Johnsons wanted something unposed and honest, so we planned around golden hour and gave everyone room to just be themselves.
