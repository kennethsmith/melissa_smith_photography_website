Featured Session

Ella's Senior Portraits

August 2024 | Cannon Beach

The Story

Golden hour on the Oregon coast. Ella brought four outfits and a college pennant for her last-minute class-of-2025 portraits — a little bit of Cannon Beach, a little bit of everything she hopes to do next.

We started at the tide pools, moved up to the dunes, and finished as the sun sank behind Haystack Rock. The salt air did the hard work; my job was just to capture a soon-to-be high school graduate at the very start of something new.
